# Decision record

Decision and status: confirmed, proposed, superseded or unresolved.
Scope/date/decision-maker: known values or unknown.
Evidence: exact source or user answer.
Rationale: supplied reasoning, or unknown.
Alternatives: supplied alternatives; label assistant suggestions separately.
Review condition: supplied trigger/date, or pending.
Observed outcome: unknown until supported by evidence.
Predecessor/change: meaningful prior decision and correcting source if applicable.
Project route: relevant hub.

Use a section in the project note when sufficient. Do not create rationale, agreement or outcomes merely to fill this template.
