# Meeting brief

Mode: preparation or recap. Status: draft for review.
Purpose, project and audience: supplied context.
Meeting date and participants: supplied values or unknown.

## Supported context

Relevant sourced facts and available evidence; distinguish live checks from dated notes.

## Agreements and actions

Confirmed decisions and assignments only. Unknown owners or dates remain unknown. For preparation, do not label proposed agenda items as agreements.

## Suggestions and open questions

Source suggestions, proposed agenda/next actions and unresolved issues, clearly separated.

## Sources and continuation

Exact appropriate source links, relevant project hub and predecessor if revising. For a public output, keep internal evidence, predecessor and hub routes in the internal hub instead; use public-safe citations or a source-limits statement.
